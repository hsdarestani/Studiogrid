# Studiogrid Demo

Interactive front-end demo for **Studiogrid – Studio Operating System**.

The demo covers the complete product scope from the specification, including:

- Multi-tenant foundation, roles, audit and GDPR
- Member management
- Tariffs, contracts and in-studio sales
- SEPA, receivables and dunning
- Accounting, invoices, DATEV and e-invoicing readiness
- Access control with RFID/NFC/QR and offline concepts
- POS, TSE adapter, DSFinV-K and Z-report workflows
- Online contracts, cancellation and withdrawal flows
- Reporting and studio KPIs
- Location analytics, PostGIS concepts and expansion scoring
- AI communication, churn prediction and AI receivables
- Lead automation, AI marketing and internal automation
- Live marketing, payments and accounting integrations
- Member app, classes, training, trainers, streaks and referrals

## Demo mode

The current implementation is a visual/interactive product demo. Third-party providers, banks, physical access hardware, certified TSE devices and legal/tax sign-offs are represented as demo integrations rather than live production connections.

## Deployment

The repository includes an automated GitHub Actions deployment for `studiogrid.smarbiz.sbs` to the configured server.
